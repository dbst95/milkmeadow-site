Milk Meadow

A private breast pump tracker for iPhone.

Milk Meadow keeps everything on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no network access, no ads, and no tracking. Your pumping log is yours alone.

Support

Questions, bugs, or feature requests — email strole95@gmail.com. It goes to a real person and you will get a reply.

Useful things to include if you are reporting a problem:

  • What you were doing when it happened
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version, shown in Settings → Version

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. The short version: nothing is collected, and nothing leaves your device.

A note on storage guidance

Milk storage limits in the app follow published U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention guidance for healthy full-term infants. They are general information, not medical advice. For questions about your own situation, please ask your pediatrician or a lactation consultant.
