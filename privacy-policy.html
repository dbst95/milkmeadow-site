Privacy Policy for Milk Meadow

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Milk Meadow does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Ever.

Milk Meadow is a breast pump tracker that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Information we collect

None. Milk Meadow has no mechanism for sending information anywhere. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

Information you create

Everything you record in Milk Meadow — pumping sessions, volumes, notes, stored milk batches, feeds, storage locations, pumps, and your settings — is written to a private database on your device. It is:

  • stored only in Milk Meadow's own sandboxed storage on your iPhone;
  • protected by your device passcode and iOS file encryption;
  • never sent to us or to anyone else, because the app has no network access;
  • included in your iPhone backup if you back up your device, in which case Apple's own privacy policy governs that backup;
  • permanently deleted when you delete the app.

We cannot see this data. There is no server for it to reach and no account attached to it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Milk Meadow schedules local notifications through iOS. These are generated on your device and delivered by your device. No push server is involved and no information leaves your iPhone. Notification permission is requested only when you switch a reminder on, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.

Exporting your data

Milk Meadow can generate CSV files of your sessions, stored milk, and feeds. These files are created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet only when you ask for them. Where they go from there — Files, Mail, AirDrop, or another app — is entirely your choice, and that destination's own privacy policy applies once you send them.

Children

Milk Meadow is intended for adults tracking their own pumping. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Milk Meadow does not connect to Apple Health and does not read or write HealthKit data. The milk storage limits shown in the app follow published U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention guidance for healthy full-term infants. They are general information, not medical advice. Please consult your pediatrician or a lactation consultant with questions about your own situation.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Milk Meadow ever changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
